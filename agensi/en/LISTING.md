# Agensi listing (EN)

## Title
Company News Research & Social Posts — topics + Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram posts

## Short description
Your agent finds fresh news in your company's industry, suggests sourced blog topics and writes Facebook, LinkedIn and Instagram posts.

## Long description
Running out of ideas for posts and blog articles? Give your agent the company's website and it builds a profile, areas to monitor and a list of trusted sources for your industry and country. Then, with one command, it searches for fresh news (regulations, events, trends), checks dates and sources and saves a report with 5–10 topics sorted by urgency. Pick a topic and it writes a ready-to-publish post for Facebook, LinkedIn or Instagram — in your company's tone, with a source link, call to action and hashtags, within the character limit.

- Any company, any industry, any country; reports and posts in the language set in the profile.
- Every topic links to its source — no source, no topic.
- Platform-aware: a 2-line hook on LinkedIn, "link in bio" and an image idea on Instagram.
- Multiple companies in one folder, switched with one command.
- Everything is plain Markdown files you can edit.

## Skills in the bundle
- **company** — adds a company from its website and switches the active company.
- **scope** — sets what to search for: your own topics or the profile's default areas.
- **topics** — finds news from the last N days and saves a report with topics.
- **post** — writes a Facebook, LinkedIn or Instagram post about a chosen topic.

## Tags
marketing, social media, content marketing, facebook, linkedin, instagram, blog, research, small business

## Example
```
/company add corner-cafe https://cornercafe.com
/topics 14
/post 2 instagram casual
```
